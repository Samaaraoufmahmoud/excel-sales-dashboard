# 📊 Sales Dashboard Analysis

## Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Dashboard developed in Excel to analyze sales performance across multiple dimensions, including product categories, customer segments, time periods, and geographic locations.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders monitor performance trends, identify profitable areas, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze sales trends over time.
- Compare sales and profit across product categories.
- Identify top-performing product subcategories.
- Understand customer segment contributions.
- Evaluate geographical sales distribution.
- Support strategic business planning through data visualization.

## 📈 Key Performance Insights

### Sales Trend Analysis
- Sales showed an overall upward trend throughout the year.
- Peak sales performance occurred during the last quarter, indicating higher customer demand during year-end periods.
- Monthly sales tracking helps identify seasonal buying patterns.

### Customer Segment Analysis
- Consumer customers generated the largest portion of total sales (50%).
- Corporate customers contributed 31% of total sales.
- Home Office customers represented 19% of total sales.

### Category Performance
- Technology achieved the highest sales revenue among all categories.
- Furniture ranked second in sales performance.
- Office Supplies generated consistent sales but lower revenue compared to Technology.

### Profitability Analysis
- Technology generated both the highest sales and profit.
- Sales and profit comparisons reveal category effectiveness and profitability.
- Profit analysis helps identify high-value business areas.

### Product Performance
Top-performing subcategories included:
- Phones
- Chairs
- Storage
- Tables
- Binders

These products generated the highest sales volume and contributed significantly to overall revenue.

### Geographic Analysis
- Sales performance varied considerably across states.
- The geographic map highlights high-performing regions and potential market opportunities.
- Location-based insights support regional sales strategies.
